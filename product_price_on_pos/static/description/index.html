Sales Price on POS Product Screen

Displays the formatted sales price on each product card in the Point of Sale product screen. Cashiers can instantly see prices without selecting individual products — making the checkout process faster and more efficient.

Price on Product Cards Formatted Currency Zero Configuration

What this module does

Enhances the POS product screen by adding the sales price directly onto each product card. The price is displayed in your configured currency format, giving cashiers and customers instant visibility into product pricing without any extra clicks.

Instant Visibility
Product prices are shown directly on each card in the POS product screen — no need to tap or select a product first.
Formatted Currency
Prices are displayed using your POS currency format, ensuring a clean and consistent look on every product card.
Plug & Play
Install and it just works — no configuration needed. The module extends the default POS ProductCard seamlessly.

How it works

The module patches the standard POS ProductCard component and extends its template to inject the sales price onto each product card.

  1. Install the module — it depends only on Point of Sale.
  2. Open the POS application and start a session.
  3. Each product card on the product screen now displays the list price in your configured currency format.
  4. The price appears prominently on the card, making it easy for cashiers to identify prices at a glance.
  5. No settings or configuration required — it works automatically for all products.
Note: The price displayed is the product's list price (lst_price) formatted using the POS currency settings.
